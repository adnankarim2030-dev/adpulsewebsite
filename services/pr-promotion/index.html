Still curious? Ask the team →
What is crisis communication management?

Crisis communication is the strategic management of messaging during an event that threatens a brand's reputation — controlling the narrative, coordinating with media and protecting stakeholder trust in real time.

Does AdPulse handle both digital and traditional PR?

Yes. AdPulse manages digital PR (online press, influencer outreach, digital media monitoring) and traditional PR (print, broadcast, press releases) under one team.

How many media outlets does AdPulse work with?

AdPulse maintains active relationships with 100+ media outlets across Pakistan, supporting consistent press placement for client campaigns.
